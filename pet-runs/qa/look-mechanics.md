# Blackthundercat Look Mechanics

Blackthundercat should communicate look direction primarily through the eyes and head turn, with secondary support from ear angle, muzzle orientation, chest/shoulder twist, and the lightning-tail tip. Do not rotate the whole sprite as a flat object.

- 000 up: eyes and nose aim upward; ears perk high; chin lifts slightly.
- 090 screen-right: pupils, nose, muzzle, and tail tip aim to the viewer's right.
- 180 down: eyelids and pupils aim down; head dips; ears soften.
- 270 screen-left: pupils, nose, muzzle, and tail tip aim to the viewer's left.

Intermediate directions should interpolate smoothly clockwise between the cardinals. Keep body scale, silhouette, charcoal fur, cyan lightning markings, alert ears, luminous eyes, and sticker/plush style consistent across all direction cells. No text, guide marks, symbols, props, shadows, glow, scenery, or detached effects.
